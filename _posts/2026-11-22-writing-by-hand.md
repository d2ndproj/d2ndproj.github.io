There is something different about writing by hand. It is slower, which sounds like a disadvantage until you try it. The slowness forces you to choose your words. You cannot delete a sentence with a keystroke, so you think before you commit.

I keep a notebook by my desk now. It is full of half-finished thoughts, lists, and sentences that go nowhere. Most of it will never become a post. But some of it does, and those pieces always feel more honest than the ones I type straight into a screen.

Maybe the point is not the notebook. Maybe the point is giving your thoughts time to arrive before you try to catch them.
