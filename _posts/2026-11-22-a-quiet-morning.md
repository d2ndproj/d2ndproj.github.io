This morning was quiet in the best way. No rush, no noise, just the sound of rain on the window and a warm cup of something in my hands.

I used to think quiet mornings were wasted mornings. Like the day had already started without me and I was falling behind. But I am starting to think the opposite is true — that a slow start is what makes the rest of the day work at all.

I read a few pages, wrote a few lines, and watched the street wake up. Nothing remarkable happened. And that was exactly the point.
