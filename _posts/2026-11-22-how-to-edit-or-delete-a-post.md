Made a mistake? Want to change something? No problem. Posts are just files, so editing one is like editing a document.

## Editing a post

1. Go to your repository at `github.com/d2ndproj/d2ndproj.github.io`.
2. Open the `_posts` folder and click the post you want to change.
3. Click the **pencil icon** at the top right of the file.
4. Change the text, then click **Commit changes**.

Or just ask me: tell me which post and what to change, and I will do it for you.

## Deleting a post

1. Open the post file in the `_posts` folder.
2. Click the **trash icon** at the top right.
3. Click **Commit changes**.

The post will disappear from your blog after the next build, about a minute later.

## A few things to remember

- Every change needs a commit. Until you click **Commit changes**, nothing is saved.
- After a commit, GitHub rebuilds your site. Wait a minute, then refresh the page to see your change.
- If you change a post's title in the file name, its link changes too. The old link will stop working, so only rename a file when you need to.
