title: "Start Before You Feel Ready"
date: 2026-11-22
---

Here is a small truth I keep relearning: you will never feel ready. Not for the blog, not for the project, not for the thing you have been thinking about for months.

Readiness is not a feeling that arrives. It is something you build by starting. The first post is awkward. The second one is easier. By the tenth, you have a habit, and the habit carries you further than motivation ever could.

So this is your sign, if you needed one. Start the thing. Write the first line. Publish it before it feels perfect — perfect is just procrastination with better branding.
