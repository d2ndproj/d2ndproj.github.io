SEO means Search Engine Optimisation. It is how you help Google find your blog and show it to people. You do not need to be an expert. A few simple habits cover most of it.

## 1. Use short, clear links

Your blog already does this. Post links look like `/starting-a-blog/` — short, no date, no `.html`. Short links are easier to read, share, and remember, and search engines like them.

## 2. Write clear titles

Your title is the first thing Google and readers see. Make it say what the post is about, in plain words. "How to Write a New Post" is good. "My Thoughts" is not — nobody searches for that.

## 3. Write a good description

Your blog has a description in `_config.yml`: "Just writing." Search engines can show it in results. If you want, change it to one sentence about what you write about. Keep it short.

## 4. Use headings

Break your post into sections with headings (`##`). It helps readers scan, and it helps Google understand what each part is about. One main idea per section.

## 5. One idea per post

A post about one thing ranks better than a post about five things. If you have two ideas, write two posts and link them to each other.

## 6. Write regularly

You do not need to post every day. But a blog that grows steadily does better than one that stops. Even one post a week is enough.

## 7. Be patient

New blogs take time to appear in search results — often weeks or months. That is normal. Keep writing, and Google will find you.

Most of SEO is just this: clear titles, clean links, and writing people actually want to read. You are already doing that.
