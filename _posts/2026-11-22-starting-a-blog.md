I have been meaning to start a blog for a long time. Not because I have something important to say, but because I like the idea of having a place where my thoughts can live outside my head.

There is something about a blank page that feels both inviting and a little scary. Inviting, because anything could go there. Scary, for exactly the same reason.

So here is the deal I made with myself: no niche, no schedule, no pressure to be interesting. Just writing, whenever there is something worth writing down. Some of it will be short. Some of it will be half-formed. All of it will be mine.

If you are reading this, welcome. This is the beginning.
